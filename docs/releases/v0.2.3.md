## Ghost Controller v0.2.3 — choose your HUD colour

White HUD lines disappear against cloud, snow or bright sky. You can now pick
the colour of everything drawn over the video.

### New
- **HUD Color** — Ghost Controller ▾ menu → *HUD Color*: White, Green,
  Yellow, Cyan, Orange, Magenta or Black, or **＋** to pick any custom colour.
  It recolours the heading tape, roll scale, pitch ladder, aircraft marker,
  speed/altitude tapes and the fps label, live. Your choice is remembered.
- The outline under the HUD flips automatically: dark under light colours,
  light under dark ones (so even black stays readable on bright footage).

### How to update
Re-run the installer, or see
[docs/UPDATING.md](https://github.com/Sai-Tolle/Ghost-Controller-/blob/main/docs/UPDATING.md):

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sai-Tolle/Ghost-Controller-/main/install.sh | bash
