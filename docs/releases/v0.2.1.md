## Ghost Controller v0.2.1 — redesigned UI and light theme

v0.2.0 shipped the connection fixes, but the new interface was left out of
that build. This release adds it.

### New look
- Apple-style interface: Inter typeface, system colours, translucent panels,
  vector icons, segmented controls and macOS-style menus throughout.
- **Light theme.** Ghost Controller ▾ menu → *Appearance*: Dark, Light or
  Auto (follows the OS). Your choice is remembered.
- New compass **heading tape** and cleaner **roll scale** at the top of the
  HUD; the pitch ladder fades at the edges and dashes below the horizon.
- Less clutter: helper captions removed; errors are shown in red.

### Also included (from v0.2.0)
- Auto connection tries every USB serial device and the Pi GPIO UART at
  57600 and 115200 baud, then listens for UDP on `0.0.0.0:14550`.
- Clear connection errors (dialout permission, busy port, baud/wiring).

### How to update
See [docs/UPDATING.md](https://github.com/Sai-Tolle/Ghost-Controller-/blob/main/docs/UPDATING.md),
or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sai-Tolle/Ghost-Controller-/main/install.sh | bash
